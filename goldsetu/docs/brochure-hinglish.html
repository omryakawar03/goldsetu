Roz ke kaam, ek jagah

Jewellery business ka hisaab, ab aur saaf.

Chhoti chhoti manual entries milkar bada workload ban jaati hain. GOLDSETU ka goal hai routine kaam ko structured, searchable aur review-friendly banana.

◌Aaj ki mushkilein

Alag registers, stock dhoondhne mein waqt, billing ke waqt rate confusion, customer history scattered, aur closing par numbers match karne ka pressure.

✦GOLDSETU ka approach

Barcode-linked catalogue, guided billing, rate history, customer-linked receipts, clear reports aur pending-sync visibility — taaki team ko kaam aur owner ko picture samajh aaye.
